echofarmer invites you to celebrate the release concert of Tiny Big Much, their first long-form mixtape. Just like the mixtape, the concert at Botanique will be a continuous stream of shape-shifting, whimsical, club-inspired music celebrating the laptop as a magical world-making machine.
Moving between intimacy, explosion, cheerfulness and melancholy, echofarmer constantly plays with your expectations while meticulously tending to the tiniest sound design details.

Expect a concert like a garden for you to explore: sometimes you’re an ant crawling through the grass, at others you’re a whole network of fungi sprawling throughout the digital soil.
